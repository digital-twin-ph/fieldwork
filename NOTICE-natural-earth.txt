Natural Earth
=============

assets/natural-earth-110m.json is prepared from Natural Earth 1:110m vector data.

  Source   https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector
  Upstream https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
  Licence  Public domain. Natural Earth states no restrictions on use.

Natural Earth asks for no attribution and imposes no share-alike. This notice is here
because a reader of a figure is entitled to know which ground it was drawn on and how
generalised that ground is, which is a different obligation from a licence one and is
not discharged by the absence of a licence.

WHAT WAS DONE TO IT
  - Four layers taken: land, lakes, national boundary lines on land, and first-level
    administrative boundary lines.
  - Attributes dropped. Only geometry is carried, so that no name or code in this file
    can be read as data.
  - Coordinates rounded to two decimal places, about 1.1 km. That is finer than the
    data's own generalisation and coarser than its stored precision.

  scripts/prepare-natural-earth.mjs is the script, and it records the same facts inside
  the asset itself.

WHAT IT IS NOT FOR
  Context, never frame. These boundaries are generalised, and disputed lines are a
  cartographic choice rather than an adjudication. Joining data to them, computing areas
  from them, or reporting results by them produces wrong answers that look professional.
  The frame is a study area the practitioner supplies.

COUNTRY OUTLINES, FOR PROPOSING ONLY (experiment 204)
  assets/natural-earth-110m-countries.json is prepared from Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0
  countries, version 5.1.2 (geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson), retrieved
  2026-11-10, public domain.

  Unlike the file above, it keeps a code and a name per country, because its one job is
  to propose which countries a study area may touch. It never decides: the share of the
  area in each country is measured on that country's national outline from
  geoBoundaries, and the practitioner confirms. Generalised to about 10 km, so the area
  is widened by 10 km before it is compared.

  - Kept: an ISO3 code (ISO_A3, else ISO_A3_EH, else ADM0_A3; France, Norway, Kosovo,
    Northern Cyprus and Somaliland carry -99 in ISO_A3), the name, and the outline
    rounded to 0.001 degree, about 110 m.
  - scripts/prepare-natural-earth-countries.mjs is the script.
