revision 8bde848d13d71602b2b32c62e5d4b4957e4a45a1,
content/outputs/soho.graphml. Original GraphML retained alongside network.json.
network.json is a derived graph database under ODbL: node IDs, x/y coordinates,
directed edge endpoints, length and LINESTRING geometry retained; other tags
omitted. 738 nodes, 1619 directed edges. Extraction date unknown. This is not
a reconstruction of 1854 streets. Obtain the original and derived data from
examples/john-snow in Fieldwork's repository or the cited pinned source.

Fieldwork's algorithms are independent conceptual adaptations. No notebook
code, finite Voronoi helper, or Python pickle is copied or executed.
Study boundary is an illustrative Fieldwork-authored reporting rectangle.
Outputs are modeled proximity/reachability, not actual pump use or causation.
