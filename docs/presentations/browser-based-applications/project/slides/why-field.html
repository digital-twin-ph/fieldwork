Why it matters in the field

What field epidemiology gains

Sensitive data stays put

Line lists and case locations are worked on where they are, not uploaded to someone else’s server.

No install rights needed

A locked-down ministry or agency laptop can still open it: it is a web page.

Works with poor connectivity

Load it once with internet; keep working when the connection drops.

Same tool everywhere

Windows, Mac or Chromebook, the trainee and the mentor see the same thing.

Four reasons this matters for our work. Data protection: the data never leaves the device unless you choose to share it. Locked laptops: many of our partners can't install software, but they can open a web page. Connectivity: field sites often have intermittent internet. And consistency: everyone in a training cohort runs the same version on whatever machine they have.
