# Calculate area

_Created 2026-11-20 · Updated 2026-11-20_

**How large the study area is.** The denominator of a density, in the unit you choose.

## What it does

It measures the study area on the curved Earth, not on the flat map: Turf's spherical polygon area, with a mean
Earth radius of 6,371,008.8 m. It passes the same study area on, with its size attached, so a later step or a map
can state it.

## What you connect

| Input | What it takes |
| --- | --- |
| **Study area** | the area to measure |

## How to use it

Choose the study area under **Inputs**. **Configure…** sets the **Area unit**: square metres, square kilometres,
hectares, square feet, acres or square miles. Nothing is written until **Apply**. **Show me how it works**, in the
dialog, measures a made-up district step by step in the unit you have chosen.

**Result** gives the area calculated at the last run.

## What it will not do

- **It will not measure on a projection.** An area measured on a flat map differs, more so far from the equator.
- **It will not subtract water or uninhabited land.** The area is the polygon's, as drawn.
- **It will not change the shape.** The area passed on is the one it was given.
