Bundled data for the Manila coastal exposure example
====================================================

Two sources, two licences. Both travel with anything derived from them.

1. Sea-level projections and the projection site list
   IPCC AR6 Sea Level Projections, version 20210809
   https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5914709
   Licence: CC BY 4.0

   `projections.json` holds 288 values for 8 Philippine tide gauges, extracted by
   validation-lab/scripts/extract_ar6_philippines.py. Values are in MILLIMETRES,
   measured against the 1995-2014 baseline. `basis` is a confidence level, not a
   workflow: the AR6 confidence output files are p-boxes over several workflows and
   carry no workflow dimension at all.

   The licence obliges three citations, and they are required by the widget that
   declares the extract:

   - Fox-Kemper, B., et al. 2021: Ocean, Cryosphere and Sea Level Change. In Climate
     Change 2021: The Physical Science Basis. IPCC AR6 WG1, Chapter 9.
     doi:10.1017/9781009157896.011
   - Kopp, R. E., Garner, G. G., et al. 2023. The Framework for Assessing Changes To
     Sea-Level (FACTS) v1.0. Geoscientific Model Development 16, 7461-7489.
     doi:10.5194/gmd-16-7461-2023
   - Garner, G. G., et al. 2021. IPCC AR6 Sea Level Projections. Version 20210809.
     doi:10.5281/zenodo.5914709

2. Health facilities
   OpenStreetMap, via the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team country export on HDX
   https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
   Licence: ODbL 1.0
   (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0

   `facilities.json` holds 194 named hospitals and clinics inside a Manila bounding
   box, together with the retrieval record that produced them. They were fetched by
   the Find health facilities widget's own retrieval path against the published
   export, so the provenance stored in the example is the widget's own. ODbL is
   share-alike: a database derived from this must be offered under the same licence
   with attribution intact.

   This is what the source records, not a facility register, and it is not complete.
   OpenStreetMap states no completeness, so an absence here is not evidence of
   absence on the ground. Pressing Retrieve in the example refreshes it.

Rebuild with:
  validation-lab/scripts/build_sea_level_example.py <hotosm_phl_health_facilities.geojson>
